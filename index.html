// What we do

Build it well. Test it hard. Share what we learn.

01

Web apps, SaaS and ERP

We build web apps, SaaS products, and ERP-style business tools. We keep them fast and lightweight, with no bloat, and secure from the first line of code.

We practise on our own test setups and deliberately vulnerable labs, read advisories, and keep notes so others can learn from them too.

// Work with us

Small team, clear ground rules.

We are a small team, so we take on a few things at a time and would rather do them properly.

Before any work starts, we talk it through and agree what is in scope.

  • We test only systems you own or have permission to test
  • We agree the scope in writing before we start
  • We keep what we find private until it is fixed
  • We tell you honestly if a project is not a good fit
  • We are an independent team, not a certified firm
  • We share who we are with you before a project starts

// Responsible disclosure

If we find a bug, here is what we do.

Our promise when we spot a weakness

  • We report it privately to the owner first, never in public.
  • We touch only what we need to confirm the problem, and we do not keep or share any data we see.
  • We give the owner reasonable time to fix it, normally 90 days, before we write about it.
  • We never ask for money in return for silence.

If you found a problem with us

  • Email svartr@proton.me with what you found and how to reproduce it.
  • We reply within a few days and keep you updated.
  • We credit you if you want credit, and stay quiet if you do not.
  • Our contact details are also in security.txt.
// FAQ

Questions people ask us.

Who is Svartr_Labs?

We are a small team of developers and cybersecurity enthusiasts. We build web apps, SaaS, and ERP tools, study security, and help people review and fix weaknesses in their own projects.

Do you reveal who is on the team?

We keep individual names off the public site, and you can verify our work on GitHub. Once a project becomes serious, we share who we are during the first call or in the contract, along with the details needed for invoices and agreements.

Are you a certified security company?

No. We are an independent team, not an accredited or certified firm, and we do not claim to be. We are a good fit for small projects, personal apps, and learning-focused reviews. For regulated, critical, or large systems, hire an accredited firm.

Do you test systems without permission?

No, never. We only test systems that you own or have clearly allowed us to test, and we agree the scope in writing first.

What kind of work do you do?

Secure development, security testing of web apps and small setups, and learning and research that we write up and share.

Will you ask me for passwords or payment?

We will never ask for passwords, keys, or payment through this site. If a message claims to be from us and asks for these, do not trust it. Email us to check.

How can I contact you?

Email svartr@proton.me or message us on LinkedIn. Every message goes to the people doing the work.
